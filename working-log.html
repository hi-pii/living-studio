p5.js project / Concept study

Information Bubble

Colourful bubbles drift outward from a wand. Clicking a bubble bursts it and increases the chance that its colour appears again. Repeated choices gradually allow certain colours to dominate.

The interaction explores algorithmic recommendation and the formation of a filter bubble / information cocoon.

This working website preview uses plain JavaScript Canvas. Your p5.js sketch can replace it later. The log below is an unfilled record of the proposed tests; it does not claim they have been completed.
